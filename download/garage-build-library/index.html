Frequently asked questions

How is the Garage Build Library delivered?

Everything is digital. The plans and guides are files you open on your phone, tablet or computer — nothing physical is shipped. Downloads include PDF documents you can view, print and save.

How do I get access after payment?

Once your payment is confirmed, you receive your access by email — usually within a few minutes. If nothing has arrived after 10 minutes, check your spam and Promotions folders, then reach out using the contact details in your order confirmation.

Do I need welding experience?

Yes — basic experience is expected. The library is built for people who already know how to safely cut and weld steel and use common shop tools. It provides plans and planning resources, not welding instruction.

Why do some examples show centimeters?

The additional design examples come from an earlier edition and use centimeters. The starter side-table project is drawn in inches. Always verify dimensions, joints and materials against your own stock before building.

Are material costs the same everywhere?

No. Steel, lumber, hardware and finish prices vary by region and supplier, and they change over time. Use the Custom Quote Worksheet to build quotes from your actual local costs — including consumables, shop time and overhead.

Is any sale or profit guaranteed?

No. Results depend on your craftsmanship, your local market and your pricing. Nothing in the Garage Build Library is a promise of sales, income or profit.
